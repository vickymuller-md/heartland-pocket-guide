# Rural HF Pocket Guide v0.2.2

This operational-clarification patch adds a printable local-readiness page and a downloadable training pack containing 12 synthetic scenarios, responsibility and coverage worksheets, paper workflow records and respectful escalation. These resources are included in the offline cache; their presence is not evidence that a clinical team completed training.

Tier descriptions now preserve common clinically indicated goals, individualized timing and safe monitoring. Resource levels describe delivery and support, not treatment eligibility. Community, ambulatory and remote pharmacy participation and indicated financial-assistance pursuit are explicit at Tier 1 as well as higher tiers. Recognition, assessment and authorized decisions are separate; planned, urgent and advanced-HF referral contexts retain accepted-transfer responsibility.

Two versioned V3.4.1 PNG cards replace the current links for GDMT and the implementation-tier summary. Historical JPGs remain preserved. Numerical dose, risk and alert constants are unchanged. The release does not authorize prototype distribution to patients, clinical operation or an outcome claim.

The guide remains static, zero-login and reference-only, without patient-data fields. Publication and deployment are separate evidence. The previous v0.2.1 archive remains explicitly identified until the successor archive is published. Patient studies, separate ambulatory-entry definition and institutional activation remain outside this patch. The existing PWA/Astro peer-support qualification remains in README.md.
